Welcome To The Maldives

Destination > Maldives

Beautiful Beaches

Soft white sand, palm trees and clear blue water make the Maldives a dream beach destination.

Overwater Villas

The Maldives is famous for villas built over the ocean, offering beautiful views and peaceful surroundings.

Marine Life

Its coral reefs are home to turtles, manta rays, reef sharks and thousands of tropical fish.

Sunsets

The island scenery and calm ocean create stunning sunset views throughout the islands.

Best Time to Visit

The Maldives is a tropical destination with warm temperatures throughout the year.

November–April: Generally drier weather, often ideal for beach activities.

May–October: Wetter season, with more rain and wind, but visitors can still enjoy the island
