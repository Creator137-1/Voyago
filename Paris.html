Fashion

Art and Museums

The city is home to famous museums, historic art and cultural collections.

Best Time to Visit

April–June: Pleasant weather, blooming gardens and comfortable sightseeing.

July–August: Summer warmth, longer days and busy tourist attractions.

September–October: Mild weather and beautiful autumn scenery.
